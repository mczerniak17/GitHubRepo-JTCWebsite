About Me

Hi, my name is Jake Czerniak, and I am a senior at Plainfield East High School in Illinois. I am a competitive golfer with a strong passion for the game, and my goal is to continue playing golf at the collegiate level.

I have worked extremely hard over the past five years to develop both my swing and my mental game. During my last high school season, I averaged 37.6 for nine-hole matches and finished 6th in my conference out of approximately 130 players. I have also started this high school season very strongly. In my first two 18-hole invitationals, I placed 1st and 2nd individually, shooting a 68 (-3) and a 69 (-2), respectively. Our team also won both events.

This summer, I played in a lot of IJGA tournaments. My season didn’t always go the way I wanted, but I learned a lot from playing under tournament pressure and was able to win three IJGA events. Along with my high school tournament win in August, that gives me four tournament wins so far in 2026. I also learned a lot from the rounds where I didn’t play my best. Those rounds challenged me mentally and taught me to stay focused, keep fighting, and not give up on a round. I feel like that has made me a better golfer and given me more confidence that I can recover from bad shots and still find ways to make pars or limit the damage.

One of the things I value most about high school golf is my team. My teammates and coaches have become like a second family to me. I genuinely enjoy being around my team, competing together, and supporting each other. I love having people around me who push me to get better while also making the experience enjoyable, and it is very important that I find that same sense of community in a college program.

Academically, I try to take the same approach to school that I do to golf. I challenge myself with the classes I take, and getting A’s is very important to me. I have taken three AP classes, and I am taking two more this year. My unweighted GPA is 3.95, and my weighted GPA is 4.63.

My goal for this year is to stay consistent with my scores on the golf course and stay motivated with my schoolwork. I am very excited to continue developing my game throughout my senior season, and I look forward to my next chapter playing on a college team.
